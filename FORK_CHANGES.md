## How to log a change

Add a dated entry at the top of the log for every change that alters this fork,
in the same commit as the change. Upstream syncs are logged too, with the upstream
commit they merge. Use this shape:

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD

- **Area** — what changed and why (`path/to/file`, commit or PR link).
```
